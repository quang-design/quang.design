Dream Sweets is a gummy bear for ages 16 to 30. The candy market still targets kids. The brand offers zero sugar, 60 calories per bag, and xylitol as the sweetener.

Dream Sweets keeps the semblance of food, aesthetic, and joyfulness.

_Client: Dream Sweets · Project: Naming, Logo, Branding & Packaging · Location: Canada · Time: July 2021_

**Logo**

Dream Sweets is two lines of rounded type. A flat oval crosses the middle and inverts the letters inside it. Periwinkle type sits on a pale ground. White type sits on periwinkle.

![Dream Sweets wordmark in periwinkle on a pale ground and in white on periwinkle, two lines of rounded type with a flat oval inverting the letters](./dream-sweets-logo-variants.avif)

**Packaging**

The bag stays simple. The box carries the full branding. Fronts show FRUITY GUMMY BEARS, an iridescent bear, and SUGAR FREE. Callouts read 60 Calories per bag and 0.77 oz / 22 g. The side shows the wordmark, an eye, and Made in Canada.

**Audience**

Regular gummy bears are mostly for kids. Dream Sweets is for ages 16 to 30. The design is grown-up. Rounded type and periwinkle. Sugar-free, 60 calories per bag.

**Applications**

The Instagram profile is dream.sweets. The avatar is the eye. The posts show the box, the bag, and the wordmark. The bio reads Zero Sugar. Low Calories. A nice semblance of food and aesthetic.
