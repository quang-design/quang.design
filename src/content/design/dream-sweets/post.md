Dream Sweets is a naming, logo, branding, and packaging project for a sugar-free gummy bear aimed at ages 16 to 30. The work covers one simpler bag and one fully branded box, kept as two separate elements so the bag stays light while the box carries the full system.

In the presentation, people are more health conscious about sugar, including how it affects skin and metabolism. The response is a zero sugar, low calorie gummy that keeps the look and joy of candy. The January 2021 client brief positions the product at zero sugar, 60 calories per bag, and xylitol as the sweetener. The brief also claims xylitol and a chewier bite help dental health. Those dental claims are the client's, not verified here.

**Logo**

The DREAM SWEETS wordmark stacks two lines of heavy, rounded type. A flat horizontal oval cuts through the middle and inverts letter color inside the shape, so the mark reads like a lens through the name. Light and dark periwinkle variants sit side by side.

![Dream Sweets wordmark in light and dark periwinkle, stacked rounded type with a horizontal oval inverting the letter colors](./dream-sweets-logo-variants.avif)

**Packaging**

The brief asked for one pack and one box, with the bag simpler and the box holding the full branding. Pack fronts use bilingual English and French product lines, a pearlescent gummy bear, and the callouts 60 Calories per bag and 0.77 oz / 22 g. Side panels show the wordmark, a surreal eye mark, and Made in Canada.

The client brief says the candy market often targets kids. The design goal was ages 16 to 30 and packaging that reads less kid-like. Competitor product photos from the Amazon research board are not shown here.
