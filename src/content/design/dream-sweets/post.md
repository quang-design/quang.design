Dream Sweets is a sugar-free gummy bear for ages 16 to 30. The work covers naming, logo, branding, one simpler bag, and one fully branded box.

Candy shelves still speak to kids. Dream Sweets speaks to people who want the treat without the sugar load. The brand offers zero sugar, 60 calories per bag, and xylitol as the sweetener. The line is clear. Keep the semblance of food, aesthetic, and joyfulness.

_Client: Dream Sweets · Project: Naming, Logo, Branding & Packaging · Location: Canada · Time: July 2021_

**Logo**

DREAM SWEETS stacks rounded type on two lines. A flat oval cuts through the middle and flips the letter color inside the shape. Light and dark periwinkle variants sit side by side.

![Dream Sweets wordmark in periwinkle on a pale ground and in white on periwinkle, stacked rounded type with a horizontal oval inverting the letter colors](./dream-sweets-logo-variants.avif)

**Packaging**

The bag stays simple. The box carries the full system. Pack fronts lead with FRUITY GUMMY BEARS, an iridescent gummy bear, and SUGAR FREE. Callouts mark 60 Calories per bag and 0.77 oz / 22 g. The side shows the wordmark, an eye mark, and Made in Canada.

**Audience**

Most candy packaging still courts kids. Dream Sweets was built for 16 to 30, with a look that reads less kid-like on the shelf.

**Applications**

An Instagram profile for dream.sweets uses the eye as the avatar and a feed of box, bag, and wordmark. The bio reads Zero Sugar. Low Calories. A nice semblance of food and aesthetic.
