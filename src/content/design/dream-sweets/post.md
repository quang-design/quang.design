Dream Sweets is a healthier, zero sugar, low calorie alternative to traditional sugar-filled gummy bears. It is for ages 16 to 30, for people who don't want to give up their sweet tooth.

Illustration by Luong Doo

_Client: Dream Sweets · Project: Naming, Logo, Branding & Packaging · Location: Canada · Time: July 2021_

**Logo**

The logo is inspired by a mouth and teeth, the dream of eating sweets without worry. Dream Sweets is two lines of rounded type. A flat oval crosses the middle and inverts the letters inside it. Periwinkle type sits on a pale ground. White type sits on periwinkle.

![Dream Sweets wordmark in periwinkle on a pale ground and in white on periwinkle, two lines of rounded type with a flat oval inverting the letters](./dream-sweets-logo-variants.avif)

**Packaging**

The bag stays simple. The box carries the full branding. Fronts show FRUITY GUMMY BEARS, a glass gummy bear, and SUGAR FREE. The glass texture stands for purity and cleanliness, and it reinforces the low calorie, zero sugar sweet. Callouts read 60 Calories per bag and 0.77 oz / 22 g. The side shows the wordmark, an eye, and Made in Canada.

**Audience**

Dream Sweets is for ages 16 to 30. It is for people who don't want to give up their sweet tooth. Traditional gummy bears are sugar-filled. This one is the healthier, zero sugar, low calorie alternative.

**Applications**

The Instagram profile is dream.sweets. The avatar is the eye. The posts show the box, the bag, and the wordmark. The bio reads Zero Sugar. Low Calories. A nice semblance of food and aesthetic.
