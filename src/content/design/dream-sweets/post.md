Dream Sweets is naming, a logo, branding, and packaging for sugar-free gummy bears aimed at ages 16 to 30. The January 2021 brief asks for one simpler bag and one fully branded box, kept as two separate elements.

The deck says people are more health conscious about sugar, including how sugar affects skin and metabolism. It presents a zero sugar, low calorie gummy bear, and the line "We keep the semblance of food & aesthetic and joyfulness." The brief positions the candy at zero sugar, 60 calories per bag, and xylitol as the sweetener. The brief claims xylitol and a chewier bite are good for teeth, and that xylitol has been shown to reverse cavities and prevent tooth decay. Those dental lines are claims in the client brief.

**Logo**

The DREAM SWEETS wordmark stacks rounded type on two lines. A horizontal oval crosses the middle and inverts the letter color inside it. One variant is periwinkle type on a pale ground. The other is white type on periwinkle.

![Dream Sweets wordmark in periwinkle on a pale ground and in white on periwinkle, stacked rounded type with a horizontal oval inverting the letter colors](./dream-sweets-logo-variants.avif)

**Packaging**

The bag stays simpler. The box carries the full branding. Fronts show FRUITY GUMMY BEARS, a French line under the bear, an iridescent gummy bear, and the callouts 60 Calories per bag and 0.77 oz / 22 g. The side panel shows the wordmark, an eye, and Made in Canada.

![Dream Sweets bag flats, front with an iridescent gummy bear and SUGAR FREE type, side with an eye and Made in Canada](./dream-sweets-2.avif)

**Competitive landscape**

The client brief says the candy market often targets kids. The goal was ages 16 to 30, with packaging that is less kid-like.
