Powder Therapy is a Full-body Matcha Latte packaged in boxes of 10 sachet stick packs. It is a simple and light way to treat yourself better everyday, from day to night. The identity draws a symbol series from the letter O and the star, sets the name in Maison Neue, and uses Light Gray, The Neon Green, and Black. The same marks appear on the box, the stick pack, cup sleeves, stickers, and cards.

_Client: Powder Therapy · Project: Brand Identity & Packaging Design · Location: Canada · Time: 2020_

**Logo**

The symbol series comes from the letter O and the star. Fine Particle is that O drawn as a circle around one star, with ticks radiating from the star. Powder Therapy nests the star in concentric rings. Better and healthy life fills the circle with many stars.

**Type**

Maison Neue, from [Milieu Grotesque](https://www.milieugrotesque.com/), carries the voice. Built with a stronger focus on optical criteria, it pays attention to harmony, rhythm, and flow. Neutral by design, it helps the eye relax—the feeling the brand wants for its audience—whether set as a title or a paragraph.

**Color**

The palette is Light Gray, The Neon Green, and Black. Neon Green, a bright shade, stands for a focus on life and a balanced life. It signals determination toward goals—willpower, bravery, and attention.

![Powder Therapy color palette blocks for Light Gray, The Neon Green, and Black](./wangrar-vietnamese-graphic-designer-brand-identity-design-powder-therapy-9.avif)

**Applications**

The system lands on Full-body Matcha Latte boxes of 10 sachet stick packs, single-serve sachets, cup sleeves, stickers, and business cards—one showing the Instagram handle @powder.therapy. Web and social carry the same line: an Amazon-style listing grid and an Instagram profile whose bio repeats the brand promise.
