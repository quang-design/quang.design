Powder Therapy is a brand identity and packaging system for a Full-body Matcha Latte sold as sachet stick packs. The work spans naming language, a serif wordmark with a star set in the O, a three-symbol mark system drawn from that letter and star, a light gray / neon green / black palette, Maison Neue typography, and applications across boxes, sachets, cup sleeves, stationery, web commerce, and social.

The brand line on the deck is simple and light. A simple way to treat yourself better everyday. From day to night. Simple and light. The Powder Therapy.

The star in the wordmark stands for powder treatment. On the logo slide, powder is described as a dry bulk solid of very fine particles, with each particle like a star and powder like a sky full of stars. The star is meant to spark hope. The letterforms also carry a sense of depth through a 3D type treatment on the serifs.

The symbol series starts from the letter O and the star. Three circular marks carry the story. Fine Particle is a single star with radiating ticks. Powder Therapy nests the star in concentric rings. Better and healthy life fills the circle with many stars.

![Three Powder Therapy circular symbols labeled Fine Particle, Powder Therapy, and Better and healthy life](./wangrar-vietnamese-graphic-designer-brand-identity-design-powder-therapy-8.avif)

## Type

Typography uses Maison Neue from the MILIEU GROTESQUE foundry. The deck describes a stronger focus on optical criteria for a distinct grotesque that pays attention to harmony, rhythm, and flow. Maison Neue stays neutral and is meant to help the eye relax, which is the feeling the brand wants audiences to have. It is set to work in both paragraphs and titles.

## Color

The palette on the deck is Light Gray, The Neon Green, and Black. Neon green is described as a bright shade that points attention toward life and a balanced life, and as a signal of determination, willpower, bravery, and focus on goals.

![Powder Therapy color palette blocks for Light Gray, The Neon Green, and Black](./wangrar-vietnamese-graphic-designer-brand-identity-design-powder-therapy-9.avif)

## Applications

Packaging centers on Full-body Matcha Latte in boxes of 10 sachet stick packs, plus single-serve sachets, cup sleeves, stickers, and business cards with the Instagram handle @powder.therapy. Web and social mockups place the system on an Amazon-style listing grid and an Instagram profile whose bio repeats the brand line.
